Phase 3: Data Visualization

Objective

To represent Formula 1 racing data through charts and graphs that make the information easier to understand.

Visualization Development

Tableau is used to create visualizations based on the available Formula 1 data.

The visual analysis focuses on:

* Presenting racing information in graphical form.
* Identifying patterns and comparisons in the available data.
* Organizing charts for clear interpretation.
* Supporting exploration of the data through interactive visualizations.

Dashboard Integration

The visualizations are arranged within the Formula 1 Race Intelligence Dashboard to provide a unified view of the analysis.

Expected Outcome

A collection of meaningful visualizations that help users explore Formula 1 racing data.

Note: The specific charts and insights should be documented according to the visualizations present in the final Tableau workbook.
